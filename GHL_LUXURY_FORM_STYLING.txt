The landing page now visually frames the live GHL form as a premium checkout card.
The GHL form itself remains externally hosted by GoHighLevel, so its internal fields and native styling must be edited in GHL if you want them changed.
Recommended GHL form styling:
- Background: warm white / ivory
- Labels: dark charcoal
- Button: black or deep charcoal with white text, or the page's lime CTA if supported
- Border radius: 4–8px
- Button copy: PLACE MY ORDER
- Keep fields short: name, WhatsApp/phone, state, city/area, colour, quantity.
